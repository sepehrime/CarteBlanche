# Carte Blanche 🇫🇷🃏

> A smart, elegant flashcard application designed to make learning French vocabulary seamless and engaging.

## 🌟 Overview

**Carte Blanche** is a minimalist yet powerful flashcard app built to help learners build, retain, and test their French vocabulary. Whether you're mastering everyday conversational phrases, conjugations, or advanced idioms, Carte Blanche gives you the freedom (_carte blanche_) to focus on what matters most—learning the language effectively.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Flashcards:** Flip through cards effortlessly to test your recall.
- **Smart Categorization:** Organize your vocabulary by topic, difficulty, or tense.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor your mastery level over time.
- **Clean UI/UX:** A distraction-free, modern interface designed for optimal focus.
